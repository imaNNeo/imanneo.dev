    3 Description

    The IMN-10 (Iman Khoshabi, also known as imaN Neo) is a senior mobile engineer based in Amsterdam. The device builds mobile apps for a living, first in native Android and now mostly in Flutter, and it pays close attention to small UI details.

    The IMN-10 open-sourced fl_chart in 2019 and still maintains it in its spare time. On the side it makes small games, which are free to play at games.imanneo.dev.

    The nickname NeO was selected before the device had watched The Matrix. This is a coincidence and not a defect.
